The archive holds EXP-001, EXP-002, EXP-003 and EXP-005 whole, extracts of the
EXP-002 log and of EXP-004, `docs/protocol/gates.md`, ADR-0006 and ADR-0007,
and the build records of both books, each listed in its `MANIFEST.md` with the
git blob of the committed file and the SHA-256 of the deposited one. The dated
notes the designs gained after that commit, those of 2026-09-15 and
2026-09-17 among them, are not in this version; their dates are the commit
dates of this repository and nothing external.
